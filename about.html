Engineer by training, Analyst by choice

Where I come from, and how I ended up in data?

I completed my studies in aircraft maintenance engineering (mechanical faculty) with a CGPA of 3.93. However, the field turned out to be a difficult door to walk through. The professional license was financially out of reach, and the degree itself was so specialised for aviation that it didn't transfer easily elsewhere. So I did the only thing that made sense: I adapted, working across different industries and learning how things actually run behind the scenes.

In 2023, I brought all of that into data, getting certified as a Professional Data Analyst and rebuilding my toolkit around Python, SQL, and Power BI. These days I work across the whole process: pulling data, cleaning it up, building dashboards, and figuring out the story it's trying to tell. The engineering training never left me, I like precision, I trust process, and I'd rather get it right the first time than patch it later.

Right now I'm between roles and making the most of it, taking on freelance data projects and teaching others the fundamentals of data cleaning, modelling, and dashboard building. I'm actively looking for my next full-time role as a Data Analyst, ideally somewhere that's serious about putting data to work.
